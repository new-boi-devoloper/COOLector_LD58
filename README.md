## 🎮 About COOLector

**You've been invited to the party of the year! But there's one tiny problem...**

COOLector is a hilarious 2D platformer where your social status literally depends on what you collect! Race against time to prove you're the coolest cat at the party by collecting awesome items and avoiding social embarrassments.

### 🎯 The Party Vibe
- **😎 COOL Items**: Sunglasses, skateboards, vintage vinyl - boost your coolness meter!
- **🤮 LAME Items**: Socks with sandals, dad jokes, expired coupons - instant social suicide!
- **🚫 Party Hazards**: Death walls, bad investments, and fashion police!

### 🎪 Gameplay Features
- **Dynamic Character Evolution**: Watch your avatar transform from "Dog Water" to "Giga Chad" based on your collection skills!
- **Hilarious Buff System**: 
  - *Slow Down Boy!* - When life moves too fast
  - *Wild JUMP!* - For those "hold my drink" moments
- **Multiple Endings**: From "NPC Energy" to "Main Character Vibes"
- **Real-time Coolness Scoring**: Your social status updates instantly!

## 🎨 Behind the Design

COOLector was born from the universal struggle of trying to look cool at parties while actually being a hot mess. The game captures that frantic energy of arriving late to a party and desperately trying to prove your worth through questionable fashion choices and even more questionable decisions.

*"You've came late to the Party"* - Game Over Screen  
*"You've reached the Party!"* - You made it!  
*"You've invested to bitcoin"* - You done goofed
